Curio Clerk: Night Shift can be played offline without creating an account. Game progress and settings are stored locally on the device. The developer does not operate a gameplay server or cloud-save service for this version.

If the player consents, the app may use Firebase Analytics to collect coarse gameplay events and device/app information, and Firebase Crashlytics to collect diagnostics and crash information. If the player requests a rewarded advertisement, Google AdMob and its User Messaging Platform may process device identifiers, advertising data, diagnostics, consent choices, and approximate location derived from network information as described in Google's policies. Declining optional analytics or crash reporting does not disable gameplay. An unavailable or declined advertisement does not remove the player's base reward.

Players can change optional analytics and crash-reporting choices in Settings. Where required, Settings also provides an advertising privacy-options entry point. Local game data can be removed by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app.

The app is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older and is not directed to children. The developer does not knowingly request names, email addresses, precise location, contacts, photos, or user-generated content.

Third-party services may process data under their own policies:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Firebase privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
- Google advertising technologies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
