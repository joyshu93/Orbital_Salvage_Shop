Curio Clerk: Night Shift can be played offline without creating an account. Game progress and settings are stored locally on the device. The developer does not operate a gameplay server or cloud-save service for this version.

Version 1 does not include a remote gameplay-analytics or crash-reporting SDK and does not send gameplay events or crash reports to the developer. On Android app launch, UMP may contact Google to update advertising consent status. If UMP reports that ads may be requested, AdMob may initialize and preload an optional rewarded advertisement. Google AdMob and UMP may process device identifiers, advertising data, diagnostics, consent choices, and approximate location derived from network information as described in Google's policies. The player does not need to watch or accept an advertisement, and an unavailable or declined advertisement does not remove the player's base reward.

Where required, Settings provides an advertising privacy-options entry point. Local game data can be removed by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app.

The app is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older and is not directed to children. The developer does not knowingly request names, email addresses, precise location, contacts, photos, or user-generated content.

Third-party services may process data under their own policies:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Google advertising technologies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
