## Full description

Curio Clerk: Night Shift is a warm occult rule-sorting puzzle made for portrait, one-hand play on Android.

Read each night's rules and process a 12-curio shift. Send every odd little object to Repair, Storage, or Vault, and use the Hold slot when you need a moment to think. Correct decisions fill your casebook and earn coins for desk charms that make the night counter feel like your own.

All base gameplay works offline. Optional rewarded ads may offer one revive or double coins once per shift when an ad is available. You can keep playing and receive normal progression without watching an ad. No account, gameplay server, cloud save, or in-app purchase is required or provided.

English and Korean are supported.
